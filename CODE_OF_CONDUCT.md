# Code of Conduct

Human Stability Infrastructure deals with subjects that may involve difficult personal experiences, professional disagreement, institutional failure, and strong opinions about how people should be supported.

That makes respectful disagreement especially important.

Contributors are expected to challenge ideas without reducing people to the ideas they hold, the systems they work in, or the experiences they have lived through.

Criticism can be direct. It should also be specific enough to be useful.

Disagreement with the framework, its author, an organization, a profession, or another contributor is welcome when it advances understanding. Personal harassment, threats, targeted humiliation, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of private information, or attempts to intimidate someone out of participation are not acceptable.

People with lived experience do not owe the community personal disclosure in order for their perspective to matter.

Professionals do not gain automatic authority over a discussion because of credentials.

The same standard applies to everyone: explain what you see, support what you can, acknowledge what you do not know, and remain willing to correct the record.

## Reporting a concern

Concerns about community behavior should be raised where public discussion would unnecessarily expose or escalate the issue. Use GitHub's private reporting option on this repository ("Report content" on the offending comment, issue, or profile), or open a private security advisory from the repository's Security tab if the concern involves a safety or abuse issue. Do not post identifying details about anyone, including the person raising the concern, in a public issue or Discussion.

The purpose of this repository is to make difficult questions easier to examine together. The community should behave accordingly.
