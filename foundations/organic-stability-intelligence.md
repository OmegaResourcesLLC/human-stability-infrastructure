# Organic Stability Intelligence

Organic Stability Intelligence extends the stability question beyond one person.

People live inside families, organizations, neighborhoods, economies, service networks, transportation systems, healthcare systems, housing markets, educational environments, and communities. Changes in those environments can create or remove pressure even when nothing about the individual has changed.

Organic Stability Intelligence explores how those relationships behave as a living system rather than as a collection of isolated programs.

The word organic does not imply that systems naturally become fair, efficient, or humane. It recognizes that relationships change, resources move, conditions interact, and adaptations occur whether a formal plan exists or not.

The research challenge is to understand those interactions without pretending that complexity can be fully controlled.

This framework is exploratory. It should not be used to justify centralized control over people or communities simply because more information can be collected about them.
