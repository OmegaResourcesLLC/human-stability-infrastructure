# Reasoning Roles

**These files are text-based reasoning prompts and review guides, not software agents, an SDK, an API, or executable code.**

The files in this directory are portable reasoning guides that can be used by an individual, a team, a student, a reviewer, or an AI-assisted workflow to examine the same situation from different angles.

They are intentionally separated because one perspective should not dominate every question.

A coordinator may notice disconnected responsibilities. A participant-perspective reviewer may notice that the proposed solution does not match what the person actually says they need. An evidence reviewer may notice that a confident claim has little support. A safeguard reviewer may notice that a seemingly efficient process creates unnecessary authority or data collection. An implementation stress tester may notice that an idea works beautifully on paper and falls apart when staffing, timing, access, or communication fails in practice.

A role definition does not itself provide an AI system with tools, data access, memory, professional qualifications, or decision-making authority. If a role is implemented in software or used in an AI workflow, the people building and operating that system remain responsible for the permissions, data handling, review process, and safeguards around it.

Every role in this directory operates under `../safeguards/safeguard-baseline.md`. That shared baseline is the complete source of truth for project safeguards, while each role also carries a short portable safeguard summary so the core boundaries are not lost when a role is copied or used on its own.

These roles do not make final decisions.
