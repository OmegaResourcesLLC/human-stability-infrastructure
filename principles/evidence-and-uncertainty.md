# Evidence and Uncertainty

A framework becomes dangerous when its confidence grows faster than its evidence.

Human Stability Infrastructure should distinguish clearly between established evidence, direct observation, participant report, professional judgment, hypothesis, and unknown information.

Not every decision requires perfect information.

Every conclusion does require honesty about what supports it.

When evidence is weak or conflicting, the framework should preserve that uncertainty rather than forcing agreement.

When new evidence contradicts an earlier interpretation, correction should be expected.

The goal is not to make uncertainty disappear. The goal is to prevent uncertainty from being mistaken for certainty.
