# Human Authority

Tools can organize information, reveal relationships, challenge assumptions, and help people notice what they missed.

They should not quietly inherit authority that belongs to people.

Human Stability Infrastructure may eventually be used with artificial intelligence, data systems, decision-support tools, or other technology. Those systems should remain subordinate to legitimate human judgment, participant rights, professional responsibilities, applicable law, and local governance.

No AI-generated interpretation should become a clinical conclusion, legal determination, eligibility decision, sanction, sentence, supervision level, or other consequential judgment merely because the system produced it.

Technology can assist the conversation, but it should never quietly take ownership of a decision that belongs to people.
