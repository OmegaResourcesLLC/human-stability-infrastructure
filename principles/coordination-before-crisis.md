# Coordination Before Crisis

Some problems become difficult not because no help exists, but because the help exists in separate places that do not naturally move together.

Coordination is the work of helping those pieces connect.

A coordinator should not become another authority standing between a person and their own life. The role is to help make relationships, responsibilities, options, gaps, and next steps easier to understand.

Good coordination may sometimes mean discovering that no new service is needed.

It may mean repairing a handoff, clarifying who is responsible, reducing duplication, adjusting timing, finding transportation, or making sure one requirement does not unintentionally undermine another.

The goal is not maximum intervention.

The goal is enough coordination to keep manageable instability from becoming unnecessary crisis.
