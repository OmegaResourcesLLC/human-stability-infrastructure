# Field Observation

> **Privacy warning:** Do not include names, addresses, dates of birth, case numbers, medical identifiers, photographs, detailed legal identifiers, or other information that could reasonably identify a participant. Use synthetic or appropriately de-identified information.

## Context

Describe the setting in broad terms.

## What was being tested?

Identify the principle, role, template, or idea being examined.

## What happened?

Describe what was actually observed without adding interpretation that the observation does not support.

## What appeared useful?

Explain which part helped and why.

## What created difficulty?

Describe confusion, burden, disagreement, delay, duplication, missing resources, unintended effects, or other problems.

## What surprised you?

Include outcomes that did not match expectations.

## Participant perspective

If participant feedback was obtained appropriately, summarize the relevant point without including identifying information.

## What would you change?

Describe the smallest change you believe would improve the next test.

## Evidence level

Explain whether this submission reflects informal observation, practitioner experience, participant feedback, structured evaluation, formal research, or another source of information.

Do not describe informal feedback as validated evidence.
