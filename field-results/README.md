# Field Results and Implementation Feedback

This directory is intended to capture what happens when people test ideas from Human Stability Infrastructure in controlled, ethical, and appropriately governed settings.

Positive results are useful, but failures are just as important because they show where the framework needs to change, narrow its claims, or get out of the way of something that already works better.

A process that staff ignore, participants dislike, organizations cannot support, or existing methods already handle better should be documented rather than polished into success.

**Do not submit identifying participant information.** See `../safeguards/privacy-and-data-handling.md`.

Field feedback should focus on the framework, workflow, implementation condition, or observed pattern rather than creating public case files about real people.

Nothing submitted here should be represented as research evidence unless the work was conducted under the appropriate research design, ethics review, consent process, and methodological standards required for that claim.
