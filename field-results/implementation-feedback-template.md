# Implementation Feedback

> **Privacy warning:** Do not include identifying participant information. Public feedback should discuss the process, system, or framework rather than identifiable individual cases.

## Environment

What kind of organization, community, program, or setting was involved?

## Material used

Which Human Stability Infrastructure file, role, or concept did you use?

## Intended purpose

What were you trying to accomplish?

## What worked?

Describe the parts that were understandable, useful, or practical.

## Where did it break?

Describe the point where the framework became confusing, unrealistic, duplicative, unsafe, or unhelpful.

## Existing practice

Did an existing method, role, policy, or tool already address the same problem?

If so, explain the overlap.

## Hidden requirements

What staffing, time, technology, authority, funding, training, relationships, or other capacity did the idea quietly assume?

## Safeguard concerns

Did the process create privacy, coercion, surveillance, authority, bias, or other concerns?

## Recommended change

What would you change before another implementation attempt?

## Confidence

How confident are you in this feedback, and what information might change your view?
