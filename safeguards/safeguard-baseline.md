# Shared Safeguard Baseline

Every role, template, example, and future tool in Human Stability Infrastructure should operate within this baseline.

The framework is designed to support understanding, coordination, research, review, and responsible experimentation.

It is not designed or validated to independently determine diagnosis, treatment, guilt, dangerousness, criminal behavior, sentence, detention, supervision level, punishment, benefits eligibility, housing eligibility, employment eligibility, service eligibility, child-custody outcomes, or other consequential decisions about a person.

The framework should not be represented as a validated predictive instrument.

Participant disagreement should not automatically be treated as instability, resistance, deception, noncompliance, or risk.

Information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

Identifying participant information should not be placed in public repository issues, Discussions, field reports, examples, or contribution materials.

AI-generated content should remain reviewable and attributable as AI-assisted when that context is material.

Important uncertainty, missing information, and disagreement should remain visible.

Human reviewers remain responsible for consequential decisions made within their legitimate professional or organizational authority.

Nothing in this safeguard baseline overrides applicable law, professional ethics, informed-consent requirements, privacy obligations, or organizational duties.

These boundaries describe what the project supports and endorses. The open-content licenses governing the repository remain controlling with respect to copyright permissions.
