# Privacy and Data Handling

Human Stability Infrastructure does not require public disclosure of participant information.

Public repository materials should use synthetic examples or information that has been appropriately de-identified for the intended use.

Do not place identifying participant data in GitHub issues, Discussions, field reports, examples, pull requests, or other public repository content.

Identifying information includes obvious identifiers such as names, addresses, dates of birth, phone numbers, email addresses, case numbers, medical record numbers, photographs, and government identifiers.

It can also include combinations of details that make a person reasonably identifiable even when their name is removed.

The safest public example is usually a synthetic one.

Organizations testing ideas from this repository remain responsible for their own privacy, consent, security, retention, disclosure, and legal obligations.

The repository does not create a substitute privacy policy for any organization using it.
